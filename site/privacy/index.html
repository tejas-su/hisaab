Privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026

hisaab keeps your money on your phone. It collects nothing and sends nothing.

No account, no ads, no analytics, and no internet access. Something leaves the phone only when you tap to send it.

Who we are

hisaab is an expense tracker for Android made by Widgets & Co, owned by S U Tejas. In this policy, “we” means Widgets & Co and “the app” means hisaab.

What the app stores

To work out what you can spend today, the app stores what you type in:

  • your income, payday and the bills you add
  • your spends: amount, category, date and any note you write
  • your settings, such as the theme

All of it is kept in a private database inside the app on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask for internet access, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else. We have no servers, no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. We never see your data.

Android's automatic backup to Google Drive and phone-to-phone transfer are turned off for the app, so your data is not copied off the phone in the background either.

When something leaves your phone

Only when you choose to send it, in one of these ways:

  • Back up. The app saves one file to the place you pick, such as your phone’s storage or a cloud drive. Where it goes after that is up to you and the service you picked.
  • Ask AI. The app writes a summary of your spending for an AI chat app, such as ChatGPT. You see the full text first. Nothing is sent until you tap copy, share or open. The AI app’s own privacy policy then covers what you send it.

We do not sell, rent or share your data, because we never have it.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you on the day a bill is due. The app asks first, and you can say no.
  • Run at startup: to set those reminders again after your phone restarts.

The app does not ask for your contacts, location, camera, microphone, SMS or internet.

Deleting your data

You are always in control:

  • In the app, go to Settings → Your hisaab → Delete everything.
  • Or uninstall the app. Android removes everything it stored.

Backup files you saved are yours. Delete them where you saved them.

Children

The app is not made for children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Security

Your data stays inside the app’s private storage on your phone. Keep your phone locked to keep it safe, and keep backup files somewhere only you can open.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top. If the app ever starts collecting data, we will say so here and in the app before it happens.
