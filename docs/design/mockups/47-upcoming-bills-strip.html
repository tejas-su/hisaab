Home: the "Next 7 days" strip

Proposal, not built. Plan: docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-19-upcoming-bills-strip.md (decisions A to H). The strip replaces the due-soon row. Today is Mon 26 Oct 2026, payday 4 Nov, so the window is Tue 27 Oct to Mon 2 Nov. The hero is the SPEC worked example (45,000 salary, 9 days left, ₹1,430). Bill amounts and the zero-share numbers are illustrative; SPEC worked example for the warning is written in plan Task 1.

Spacing and alignment

  • The card is the same width as the hero card: both sit in the page's 20dp HisaabSpacing.gutter, so the left and right edges are shared, and the card gap is 12dp as for the nudge and Today cards.
  • Card padding is 12dp; title and total carry 8dp more, so their text starts 20dp from the card edge (the 20dp row inset). Each bill row starts at the 12dp card padding, and its icon well sits 8dp inside, so well and title also start at 20dp.
  • Row radius 18 (as DueSoonRow), card radius 28 (as the hero), icon well radius 14, row gap 8dp, no hairlines: rows are separate sunk shapes, so there is nothing to indent.
  • Rows sit on sunk on a surface card: two layers plus one well. The warning is also sunk with a 1dp line outline, glyph and words, so it never reads as a row (no chevron, no tap).
  • Hierarchy: hero number first, Day Blocks second, then this card. Title 22 Anek, rows 16/600, the weekday line and the total 14 to 15 in the muted neutral. Weights used: 400, 600, 700.

Tap targets and states

  • Each row is one tap target: full card width, at least 56dp tall (48dp is the floor), grows with text. Chevron is decoration, not a separate target.
  • Changing bill: the word "about" before the amount. Card bill: "pay by Fri" under the name, amount in the muted neutral, left out of the total. Saving: same row as a bill, sub line "Put aside" plus the weekday ("Put aside · tomorrow").
  • Marigold is not on the strip. No red, no green. The warning is words plus a glyph.
  • Page scrolls; the card has no inner scroll. At 1.5x text the rows grow and "about" drops above the amount in narrow windows (frame 4).
  • Two-pane: I assumed same strip, same column. I did not find a Home two-pane sketch in the reference mockups, so there is none drawn here.

TalkBack

  • Row: "Electricity, about 1,200 rupees, Thursday. Opens the bill." One node per row, amount spoken in full (no "₹", no "L").
  • Card bill: "Credit card, 6,400 rupees, pay by Friday. Opens the bill." Tomorrow: "Tuesday" is spoken as "tomorrow".
  • The card is a group read in order: title "Next 7 days", total "10,200 rupees goes out", warning (if any), then rows. The glyph is hidden from the reader; the sentence carries the meaning.
  • The warning is read after the title and before the rows.

Open questions for the owner

  1. Cap the rows? Six rows is long. Option: show 4 and a "+2 more" row that expands in place. Drawn uncapped (frame 4) because a cap adds a tap and a hidden bill.
  2. Chevron or none? Drawn with a chevron to match the old due-soon row and say "opens". Without it the row is calmer but looks less tappable.
  3. Total wording. Drawn "₹10,200 goes out" with the card bill left out. Alternatives: "₹10,200 to pay", or "₹10,200 goes out, card bill extra" (more honest, longer).
  4. Also for content: the card bill shows an amount that is not in the total. Should the row say "not counted" or just rely on "pay by"?
