Pace chart built: D129, D133

The Spends pace chart (FR-SP-3), three ways. Before is the chart before D129, with a straight steady ramp. D129 (built) is the fix that shipped: the steady line steps up on each bill's day, so a bill no longer reads as overspending. Proposal adds three things on top, built as D133: the gap at today as a number, where this pace ends up, and three dates under the plot. Everything is drawn with tokens only (ink, ink2, hairline, one marigold dot); no red, no green.

A · Worked example, Mon 26 Oct

Rent ₹8,200 posted 5 Oct, ₹18,950 of food on 20 Oct, ₹420 today. Electricity ₹1,200 is still to come on 29 Oct. The verdict is the same before and after D129, but the steady line now meets the rent step, and the proposal says how much room there is and where the month ends up.

B · The bug: Mon 5 Oct, the day rent goes out

Nothing has been spent except rent. Before D129 the straight ramp was at ₹2,903, so ₹8,200 read Spending faster and stayed "faster" until 9 Oct. With D129 the steady line has the same step, so it reads Spending slower. The forecast is hidden because there are fewer than 3 days of history (BR-7, E < 3), and the 4 Oct label gives way to Today where they would overlap.

C · Spending fast: ₹9,000 more on 22 Oct

The same cycle with ₹9,000 more spent on Thu 22 Oct, ₹36,570 by today. Pace = (18,950 + 9,000) / 22 ≈ ₹1,270 a day. With electricity on the 29th the total passes ₹45,000 on Sun 1 Nov, the same date BR-7 gives Home's range line ("At this pace it runs out on 1 Nov"). The forecast stops where it crosses the budget, with a ring and the date.
