Screen entrance motion proposal

How screens arrive, drawn from case study 24. Today the hero is alive (fill in, sheen, drain), but the rest of the app cuts: the dock swaps screens with no transition, Spends pops in from a blank frame, and a new entry appears in the list at once. Each moment below has 3–4 versions side by side. Tap a phone to replay it, or use a section's Replay all to compare them in sync. Slow ×4 makes the timing readable.

1. Home appears

Home is opened dozens of times a day, and the hero already carries two motions each time (fill in on calm, sheen 1.4s). The question is how much the cards under it may move without stealing the focal point.

2. Spends appears

Spends is opened about daily, and its four cards are what you came to read. Today it draws nothing while loading (SpendsLoading() => []), then everything pops in at once. Every version below has the same 160ms load.

3. Switching from the dock

Each replay taps Spends, waits, then taps Home. Home's fill in and sheen play again on return in every version, as D91 and D106 already say. Note how C stacks motion on every switch.

4. A save lands in the Today list

₹250 for an auto, filed under Travel. The tray closes, then (D91) the yellow pulls back, the number rolls 1,430 → 1,180, and the new row joins the list. D shows a delete with Undo bringing the row back along the same path.

For you to pick

  • Home: B (one quiet group) or C (card cascade)? A is today; D is shown only to see why per-row cascades are rejected.
  • Spends: B (even cascade), C (fade only) or D (the pace card leads and travels further)?
  • Dock: B (fade through) or D (a short sideways slide)? Should Spends' cascade replay on every switch (C), or only on its first frame of a session?
  • Save: B (row grows with the drain) or C (row waits until the drain settles)?
