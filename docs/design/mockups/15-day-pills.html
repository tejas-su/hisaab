Day pills Proposal, not agreed in SPEC

The hero's Day Blocks redrawn as capsules: today takes two width shares, the fill is a flat level in a capsule tank, and each everyday change has one quiet motion. Worked example from SPEC BR-4: Mon 26 Oct, 9 days to payday 4 Nov, A = ₹1,850, ₹420 spent, ₹1,430 safe today. Tokens are HisaabColors light and dark; springs are critically damped (no overshoot) at snap 0.25s, standard 0.35s and calm 0.55s. Press any card: it sinks to 0.97.

1 · Home, in context

360dp phone, 20dp gutters, so the hero is 320dp and the pill row 226dp (content 280 − flag 44 − gap 10). Hierarchy holds: the number, then today's pill (the only colour on screen), then the range line, then the Today list.

2 · Geometry

Today = 2 shares, at least 24dp. Future pills share the rest equally. Gap 6dp, 3dp above 16 days. If a future pill would be under 6dp, draw 7 pills and "+N". Height 40dp, radius = min(w, h) / 2. The level is clipped by the capsule, so its top is always flat; once anything is spent the level stops at 0.86 (BR-6 change: today 0.9), so 4dp stays clear inside the 1.5dp edge. In light a 1dp displayEdge line marks the level's top.

3 · States, light and dark

State is fill, outline, hatch and words. Marigold appears only on today's pill. Every state keeps its words: the title, the amount, the captions and the range line.

4 · Micro animations

Each plays once per cause, never loops. Replay runs it again in light and dark. Tick Reduce motion at the top to see the fallback.

1 · Home appears

Today's level rises from 0 to 0.773 on calm. With it, one diagonal band of displaySheen crosses the card: 1.4s, the first quarter a wait, then the sweep curve.

Reduced motion: the level is there at once; no sheen.

2 · A spend (₹300)

After the Add tray has closed: the level falls 0.773 → 0.611 on standard and the number rolls ₹1,430 → ₹1,130 in the same frame. The spent slice stays as marigold at 50%, holds 500ms, then falls on calm.

Reduced motion: a 150ms crossfade to the new state; no trail.

3 · Over today

The level falls on standard, uncovering the hatch, and the edge crossfades from the today edge to a displayInk2 outline. The trail holds 500ms; as it starts to fall, the future pills shrink on calm and their ghost outlines appear: cause, then effect. No shake.

Reduced motion: a 150ms crossfade to the end state.

4 · New day

Midnight, Mon → Tue. Yesterday's pill loses its marigold and collapses to zero width at the start edge while it fades. The others slide and widen on standard, and tomorrow widens into today. Then today fills in, with the sheen (#1).

Reduced motion: a 150ms crossfade.

5 · The morning after an underspend

Mon ended with ₹300 unspent. The future pills rise from 0.75 to full height, staggered start → end (40ms apart) on calm; the number rolls ₹1,850 → ₹1,887. The note crossfades in after the last pill lands.

Reduced motion: a 150ms crossfade to the end state.

6 · Payday morning

Wed 4 Nov, a new 30-day cycle. The last day's wide pill leaves; the new cycle's pills slide in from the start edge, staggered 45ms, on calm, then today fills in. 30 days don't fit at 6dp each, so 7 pills and "+23".

Reduced motion: a 150ms crossfade.

Rejected

  • Per-pill taps: 18dp targets, and the card is already one tap target (FR-HO-7).
  • Scrub gesture: hidden, and it fights the scroll.
  • Pulse or glow loops: motion that never ends is noise; delight scales inversely with frequency.
  • Shake on over: reads as an error and contradicts "It happens".
  • Bounce or jelly: springs stay at damping 1.0, no overshoot.
  • Animated hatch: a moving stripe pattern is a loop and a vestibular trigger.
  • Marigold on anything but today: one signal colour.
  • Confetti: celebration for an everyday save cheapens it.
  • Haptic on drain: the save already ticks; a second buzz for the level is noise.
