The missed-log line signed off 2026-11-13 (08 §8)

When you spent on something you usually spend on and the day has gone by without logging it, Home asks once, in its quiet nudge slot: Spent on Food today? Usually around 1 pm. One tap opens the Add tray at step 1 with the your usual pill carried, so the usual answer is two taps. Ignoring it is free: it asks at most once a day, has no ×, no count, no badge and no marigold. Worked example: Mon 26 Oct, 4:10 pm (the Food window ended at 2:00, plus 90 minutes is 3:30), hero ₹1,430, ₹420 spent (Travel ₹300, Chai ₹120), nothing in Food. Light and dark at 360dp, and one frame at 320dp with text at 1.5×.

Colour: the line is sunk with a surface icon well and ink / ink2 text, the same as the due-soon row. It is a question, not an act, so marigold stays on today's bar and the Add key.

Problems found in the docs

  1. 1. The line looks like the due-soon row, and that is a risk. 08 says the line is a sibling of DueSoonRow, but then a due bill ("goes in on Thu") and a question about a habit are two different asks in an identical shape. The words differ, and a question mark is the only cue. Kept identical on purpose (one slot, one voice), but the icon well carries the category glyph, and for Spent on Food today? that is the same Food glyph as the Today rows beneath it, so the line can read as one of the entries. A sibling of WaitingBillRow has the same issue. Suggested: nothing now; hallway-test it (blind-read B12 asks about feeling, not recognition).
  2. 2. The line's glyph aligns with the hero, but its well does not align with the Today wells. 08 says the 12dp (HisaabSpacing.m) inset lines the well's glyph up with the hero and Today list. With a 20dp page gutter the 24dp glyph does start at 40dp, the hero's text edge and the Today wells' left edge, but the surface well around it starts at 32dp, 8dp left of those wells. Visible in section 1: the line's well sticks out 8dp to the left of the list's wells. It is the existing due-soon geometry (mockup 29), so I kept it; moving all nudge rows to a 20dp inset would align the shapes but shrink the text column by 8dp. One decision for the shared constant (design-system).
  3. 3. The tap target is the whole row; the chevron says "opens", but at 1.5× the row is five lines. At 320dp and 1.5× the line is "Spent on Food" / "today?" plus "Usually around" / "1 pm.", about 120dp tall, a quarter of the first screen above the dock. 08 says the category name is never cut and that is kept, but there is no limit on height. It still beats a two-line cap that would ellipsise the category.
  4. 4. "Home after (line gone)" has two meanings. After a save the Today list gains a row and the hero drains; after Back without saving the slot just collapses and the list moves up. Both are drawn (section 5). The collapse moves the Today list up by 72dp (56 + 16), the one reflow in the flow. The doc says it "joins the Home reflow and finishes no later than the drain" but not what Back does: 150ms crossfade or standard motion. For design-motion.
  5. 5. The priority strip shows the deliberate gap in the slot rules. When a higher line clears mid-day the slot collapses empty and the next line does not slide in (03 section 1.2). Visually that means Home can be quieter after a bill is paid than a minute before; that is intended (no second ask) and drawn.
