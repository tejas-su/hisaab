Spends graphs, v2

The owner liked mockup 44, so this keeps its four ideas and fixes what made the screen long and hard to read. The pace card now holds the comparison. The split bar puts Saved between two solid blocks, so it never melts into the track. Each category row opens to what changed and its biggest entries, one at a time. The months strip is the last card and shows five months on a phone. The month story's card sits right under Ask AI. Words come from the copy deck, and every must-fix in the accessibility review is applied. Everything below is live: change the cycle, the text size, the width. The two phones are the same state in light and dark.

What changed from 44, and why

  1. The comparison sentence moved into the pace card, under "Spent so far". It was a 56dp card holding one line. It is the same news as the number above it, so they now read together. Finished cycle: "₹7,500 less than in August." First cycle: no line, and nothing in its place. Weekly cycles say "the week before". Under ₹100 apart reads "Same as".
  2. The split bar's blocks run Bills, Saved, Everyday, then what is not spent. In 44, Saved sat beside the empty track, where a 2% outline vanishes, and in the over-income state the striped tail covered it completely. Now it is always a bounded outline between two solid blocks, 12dp wide at least, and not drawn at ₹0.
  3. Everyday uses a new fill token, fillSoft (#787169 light, #858077 dark): 3.7:1 and 3.6:1 against the track. 44's soft fill failed 3:1 (2.7 and 2.9) and was never a token. The track has a 1dp ink2 edge, and so does the "Not spent yet" swatch.
  4. The split card has five numbers, not seven. "Money in ₹45,000" is the heading. The answer comes first and biggest ("₹16,430 not spent yet"), then "And ₹1,000 saved." The key shows amounts for Bills and Everyday only, since the sentence above already says the other two. One line defines Everyday. TalkBack reads the answer first.
  5. Where it went: a short headline ("The most went on Food.") says it in words without repeating numbers. The change against last cycle now shows only on the row you open, as its first line, so closed rows stay as built (plus a caret). One row open at a time, none open to start. A category with 3 entries or fewer shows all of them and drops the link.
  6. The months strip is the last card, with the accessibility fixes. Every month is a full 48dp-wide, 48dp-tall slot. At phone widths it shows 5 months, since 6 need 288dp inside the card (a phone gives 280 or 240). Picking a month uses the same look as picked chips (filled pill), and keyboard focus is a separate ring. A picked month reads out in one line below, and that line is hidden from TalkBack because each month's own label already says it. Bar labels and "Money in ₹45,000" are real text under the chart, not painted. The summary now counts "left over" on the same measure as the bars (spent plus saved against money in), so words and picture agree.
  7. Words from the copy deck. "Not spent yet" while the cycle runs, "Left over" once finished, "More than came in" for over, "Money in", never "income", "earned" or "still free". The chart axis in the pace card is the built chart's ("₹20K"), unchanged.
  8. The month story card (SPEC FR-SP-7, D193 and D194) sits directly below Ask AI on finished cycles, and as a slim row on days 1 to 7 of a new cycle. The split bar is the same component in both features (see "Month story" below).
  9. Screen length: about 590dp longer than today's screen on a running cycle, against about 900dp for 44 (hand estimates, table below; the live phones print the mockup's own measure).
  10. Fixed in passing: Aug's saved sliver hidden under stripes (44); one income line for every month (now per month); the strip's picked bar had the same look as keyboard focus.

Months strip: does it earn its place, and what form

  • Yes, last, and smaller. It is the only thing that answers "am I doing better than before" over more than one month. People ask it after the other questions, so it sits at the end. It shows with 2 finished cycles and with money in (the "left over" test needs it).
  • Form: a title and a one-line count ("Money was left over in 3 of the last 4 months."), 5 bars (6 only where the card is 288dp inside), month labels in real text, a dotted money-in line per month, one caption, and one read-out line for the picked month. About 300dp, the same as 44's strip, but with fewer words and no painted text.
  • Same measure everywhere: a bar is spent (solid) plus saved (outlined cap), against that month's money in (dotted). "Left over" in the count means the bar stayed under the line. The cap has a 5dp floor so it stays a box; amounts live in the read-out, not above the bars.
  • Current cycle: dashed outline, "so far" and a marigold dot with an ink ring below it (the same dot the pace chart uses for today). Past the line: striped.
  • Selection and focus: the picked month is a filled ink pill with page-colour text; focus is a 2dp ring round the whole slot. Never the same.
  • Large text (150% and up): the columns become rows (month, bar, amount), 48dp each.

Open questions for the owner

  1. Saved sits next to Bills, so the spoken order is Bills, Saved, Everyday, not the copy deck's Bills, Everyday, Saved. It keeps Saved findable and visible when you are over. Is that fine, or do you want Saved last?
  2. Five months, not six. A phone's card is 280dp inside and six 48dp targets need 288. The strip says "Last 5 months". Fine?
  3. Tapping a month reads it; "Open" goes there. That is one extra line in the read-out. Or should a tap open the month and drop the read-out line (about 40dp shorter)?
  4. Day bars in the month story: keep "darker means past the share", or hatch it like everything else?
  5. Hierarchy on finished months. Ask AI then the month story are two action cards above the first number you see. On Home the number comes first. Specced as is (FR-SP-7); is it right that finished months open with two cards before any money?
  6. No money coming in: the copy deck repeats the spend in the split card ("₹27,570 spent") right under the pace card's total. Keep both, or let the split card be just the prompt?
  7. Where it went headline: "The most went on Food." is a line (21dp) that repeats row 1. Keep it, or drop it to save the line?
