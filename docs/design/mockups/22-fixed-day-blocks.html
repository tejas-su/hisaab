Day Blocks, as 4 or 5 fixed bars proposal

The owner's brief: the Day Blocks don't look good. Redesign them with 4 or 5 fixed bars. Today they draw one block per day left (BR-6). In a 234dp row that means 40dp blocks at 5 days, 21dp at 9, 11dp at 16, and at 28 a fall back to 7 blocks and "+21". The row changes shape every day of the cycle and only looks right near payday. Every option below draws the same 4 or 5 bars all cycle, and the payday flag stays where it is. Try the switches above; 9 days on track is the spec's worked example.

Recommendation F2, 5 bars

F2 · Big today, three later days and a stack. Today is the only block that changes during the day. At double width its marigold drain, the 50% trail (D91), the slow-down sliver and the hatch are all big enough to read at a glance. The three later blocks still say "every day gets a share", and they shrink together when today is overspent (BR-6). The stack says how many more days without counting slivers. All five bars are the same size on day 1 and on day 28, so the hero looks the same every morning. Under 5 days, the stack goes first and then the later blocks, so the last day is one wide block next to the flag. F1 is the close second if a wide today feels too loud: it changes the least code. F3 is the clearest gauge, but it drops "a block is a day". That idea is the hero's whole concept, so it's shown only for comparison.

Plan, if F2 is picked

  1. Docs first: rewrite SPEC BR-6 "Number of blocks" and "Widths". There are always min(D, 4) day blocks plus a stack of D − 4 when D > 4, and today takes two slots' width. Update UI_DESIGN §5.2, mockup README and a DECISIONS row. The "under 6dp" collapse rule goes away. → verify: SPEC, UI_DESIGN and this page agree.
  2. Layout, test first: DayBlocksLayout.of returns fixed slots: today 2 units, later blocks 1 unit, stack 1 unit, 6dp gaps, and the same slot widths for every D ≥ 5. → verify: day_blocks_layout_test at D = 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 28, 31 and at every TestWindows width. No block goes under 24dp at 320dp.
  3. Data: DayBlocksData keeps count, and the stack count is derived from it. The mapper doesn't change, since todayFill and laterHeight still come from BR-6. → verify: the existing mapper tests pass unchanged.
  4. Painter: draw today wide, the later blocks and the stack (two offset outlines behind an idle card, with "+N" in Anek Latin tabular). States: the later blocks and the stack shrink to laterHeight with the ghost outline. In over-cycle, everything is outlined. → verify: painter tests per state. The trail and fill-in tests are re-measured on the wide block.
  5. Labels and TalkBack: the caption row becomes "Today" under today, short weekday names under the later blocks, and nothing under the stack. The hero's spoken sentence keeps "N more days". → verify: a11y tests, plus labels fit at 320dp with text at 200%. Past 1.3× scale, fall back to the current two captions.
  6. Motion: at midnight the stack deals the next day into the row (slide left on standard, "+N" rolls down by 1), and reduced motion crossfades. The assemble on first launch deals out the five slots. → verify: replay on a device, with Reduce motion on and off.
  7. Done: flutter analyze clean, flutter test green, scripts/changed_lines_coverage.sh ≥ 90%, and checked on the moto g45 at 9 and 28 days with FakeClock. Existing tests that assert one block per day need the owner's OK to change.
