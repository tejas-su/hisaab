Money coming in proposal

Nobody can change their income after onboarding today. SPEC FR-SE-1 promises a Money coming in row, but it was never built (D132). This mockup draws it with mockup 27's paper slip, with two rows: Amount and When it comes. Research is in case study 20.

Two rules:

  • A new amount takes effect from the next payday. Pay already received stays as it came, so today's number doesn't jump.
  • A new payday keeps this month going until that day. The current cycle runs until the day before the new payday, so nobody who was just paid lands on "No income".

Today is Mon 26 Oct 2026, with ₹45,000 on the 4th. Each frame is shown in light and dark at 360dp. Slice 1 marks the amount and Slice 2 marks when it comes.

For you to decide

  • Weekly pay. Drawn: the question asks for the monthly amount, as onboarding does, and the slip shows "about ₹10,384 a week" under it (D26). The alternative is to ask the weekly amount actually paid.
  • Change this month too (frame 4). This is for a mistyped amount at setup. It shows only when this cycle's pay was added by the rule and hasn't been edited, and it edits that one entry, with Undo. Without the chip, the fix is to edit the entry in Spends. Dropping the chip keeps the "never ask this one or all future" principle pure.
  • Slice 2 needs a new rule in the spec (the bridge cycle in frames 5 and 6). The income rule then needs a start date, which means a database schema change. Edge case E15 (the longest cycle) grows to about 62 days.
  • New payday is today. The first new payday is next month, never today, so pay can't be added twice. The footer adds "Got paid today? Add it with Add, then Got."
