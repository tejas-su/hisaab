What this mockup decides (for design-ui; the docs left these open):

  • Foot row = a text row on the page layer (section 1). The sunk 56dp row is the alternative, shown small, and is rejected on the layer rule: sunk is for wells and the segmented track only.
  • The sheet is the bill sheet's anatomy (mockup 27): grey bg sheet, one display card holding the amount and the two-line slot (no torn edge: it is not a bill), then the shared keypad and the one marigold key.
  • Marigold appears only on Fix it, as in every mockup: fill signal with a 1.5px displayEdge line (the house SignalKey), not the "marigold on ink" the interaction doc writes.
  • Direction is words only: less, more, Spent, Got. No sign, no red, no green.
Key states are always drawn in both.

1. Home: the foot row and the install line

Recommendation: a text row. After the last Today row: label Fix my number in ink2 Mukta 600 15/20, a 18dp chevron, 48dp tall, no fill, no icon well, no second line, no count. The label starts at the section titles' 4dp inset and the chevron ends on the same edge as the row amounts (20dp in), so it shares two edges with the list above it and moves nothing else. It is quiet because it has no container, and it cannot be mistaken for the nudge slot, which is a surface card with a sunk well. At 6.2:1 (light) and 7.3:1 (dark) on bg the label passes 4.5:1 with room.

Why not the sunk row (small frame): (1) UI_DESIGN §3 keeps two layers, bg then surface, with sunk only inside wells and the segmented track; a sunk card on the page would be a third layer. (2) It is the same height as the nudge slot (56dp) and has an icon well, so it reads as a second, permanent nudge, which is exactly what the IA doc's guard-rail 1 warns about. (3) It is the loudest thing under the list, louder than the list's own rows, for a door that most users never need. DOC ERRORIA §2.1 says the nudge slot is "a sunk 56dp row". In mockup 40 and DueSoonNote it is a surface card (min 56dp) with a 36dp sunk well. The conclusion (do not match it) still holds, for a different reason.

The install line uses the slot's own shape. 7. A narrow window with big text: 320 × 568 at 1.5× text

Budget. Status 28, handle area 20, keypad 4×48+3×8 = 216, action key 48+16, bottom 20: about 350dp is fixed, leaving about 170dp for the title, amount and slot, which need roughly 100 + 8 + 16 + 72 + 8 + 150 + 14 = 370dp. So the body scrolls above the fixed keypad (04 §7). The frames are scrolled to the end. Keys shrink from 64 to 48dp (the tray's rule), the action key to 48dp, and the labels never truncate: Fix it is a full-width key (see problem 1 below), Yes, add ₹30,000 fits one line. The amount is capped at 1.25× (assumption). PROBLEMScrolled to the slot, the typed amount is off screen. The slot alone is 150dp beside a 168dp window, so the amount (about 70dp) and the title scroll away; the user presses digits and cannot see them, which breaks the keypad's one job. Scrolled to the top, the slot is hidden instead. Recommend pinning the amount above the keypad, outside the scroll, and scrolling only the title and the slot under it (about 100dp left for the slot: it scrolls, the amount does not), and auto-scrolling the slot into view on the first digit (150ms, instant under reduced motion). Otherwise 320×568 at 1.5× needs the slot cut to three lines; the copy deck's longest lines cannot do that.

8. Two-pane and tabletop

Placement of the sheet. I have not been able to verify how the bill-sheet route lays out on medium and expanded windows today, so this is the arrangement Fix my number needs from it. Medium or expanded, flat: the sheet is a centred sheet, 560dp wide at most, bottom-anchored, with the scrim over the app; inside, the same stack as the phone. Expanded and wide (two-pane, as the Add tray): the title, amount and slot form one pane and the keypad with the action key the other, so the preview never sits under the thumb's keypad. The guard replaces the keypad pane (and is a 56dp-key pair at the top of its pane, centred vertically in the pane: here the "hole" does not appear because the pane is the key area). Tabletop (fold horizontal): title, amount and slot sit in the upper half (the screen the user reads), the keypad and action key in the lower half (the flat half the thumbs use); the fold line falls between the slot and the keypad, nothing straddles it. All three keep the 20dp inset, the 16dp gaps and the same tokens. design-platform to confirm and test at every TestWindows size.
