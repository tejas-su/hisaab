Today bar and days rail proposal

The owner picked B1 · Bites with the days rail (mockup 24), and asked that Today and This week be the same height so the card never resizes. So the hero has one 88dp chart slot, where Today's bar and the week bars take turns, and one days rail under it that stays put in both modes. The rail also holds the payday flag, so the week bars get the full width. Tap the first hero to switch modes; the line under it measures the card each time.
