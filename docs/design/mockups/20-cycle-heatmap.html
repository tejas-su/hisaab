Cycle heatmap options Proposal, not agreed in SPEC

The owner's idea: "something like GitHub itself, a heatmap style of expenditure". The grid is GitHub's: a column is a week, a row is a weekday (Mon at the top, M W F on the start side), with each week's Monday date above its column. It holds the whole pay cycle, so it is always 7 rows (124dp with its labels) whatever the cycle length. Past days are history, today is the one marigold cell (with the 1.5dp displayEdge ink edge in light) and drains as you spend, future days are idle cells running to the ⚑ payday cell, and days outside the cycle are not drawn at all (Mon 5, Tue 6 Oct and Sun 8 Nov). No red, no green, no streak counts, no per-cell taps.

Numbers. As mockup 19: payday Sat 7 Nov, so the cycle is Wed 7 Oct to Fri 6 Nov (31 days, 5 week columns). Today is Thu 29 Oct (9 days left), Thu 22 Oct (16) or Sat 10 Oct (28). A = ₹1,850, ₹420 spent today, so ₹1,430 is left. Past spends are made up and read against a ₹1,850 share. The example has 3 over days (Fri 9, Sat 17, Fri 23), 2 days at ₹0 (Tue 13, Tue 27) and 3 days with no entry (the Sundays 11, 18 and 25).

A gap in the data. The app has no record that tells "spent ₹0" from "didn't log": both are a day with no entries. Every option below draws them differently, but shipping that needs a new rule (an "I spent nothing" mark, or "Home was opened that evening"). See the open questions at the end.

1 · Cell encoding for past days

Each option inside the etched hero (placement P1) at 16 days left, Thu 22 Oct: 15 past days, today, 15 future days and the flag. The key under each card names every cell kind. E1k is my adjustment of E1, added because E1 gives the fill two opposite meanings in one grid.

2 · Placement

Each placement in a full 360dp phone, at 9 days left, Thu 29 Oct, with the surrounding screen. All use E1k. The dock's Add key and the wordmark dot are the screen's other marigolds, as built (they mean act and the brand).

3 · Recommended: P2 + E1k, states and motion

The "This cycle" card on Spends, with the hero left as mockup 19's V1 dot ledger. States first, then six motions, each with its own Replay. Springs are critically damped (snap 250, standard 350, calm 550ms, no overshoot); the amount rides the same spring as the cell; the drained cell lands with a 3% squash. With Reduce motion on, every replay is a 150ms crossfade to the end state.

Recommendation: P2 + E1k. The hero keeps answering "how much today?" in 293dp (19's V1), and the GitHub grid lives on Spends as "This cycle", where looking back belongs and where the switcher already pages through earlier cycles. E1k gives every cell one meaning, the day's money, so today's marigold settles into history at the same level and simply cools to grey. Ranking: P2 › P3 › P1; E1k › E1 › E4 › E3 › E2. If the owner wants the grid on Home, P3 is the version to test: it keeps history off the first screen, but costs 82dp for what V1 says in 84.

Open questions. (1) How does the app know a day was ₹0 rather than not logged? An "I spent nothing" mark adds a tap (outside the Add flow, but still a prompt); "Home was opened that evening" is a guess. Without one, merge them and draw both as not logged. (2) At 12dp tall, a ₹60 spend moves today's level 0.4dp; D91's 0.9 cap makes any spend a 1.2dp band. Enough? (3) Blind-read E1k against E1 with 5 people: "what does a tall grey bar mean?"
