Spends empty state: illustration concepts

The owner finds the empty Spends card bland and wants a picture "as done for the onboarding screens". Each concept below uses the D144 Charco Plus grammar exactly: three scribbled rocks in charcoal, taupe and one marigold rock, one ink object with spray stipple, sparkles, no faces. It is static and decorative (ExcludeSemantics). The tone is calm: nothing is wrong and nothing is celebrated. Every frame is the real 360dp Spends screen. The left column is the current cycle ("Nothing spent in November yet." with See every entry, D148). The right column is a past cycle ("Nothing was spent in September."), shown without the link because that cycle has no entries.

Placement: three options for the recommended concept

Same picture, same sentences, three homes. The card stays one SpendsCard in all three.

Recommendation

Concept 1, Potli held up, placed in the empty space below the card, centred in the gap above the dock.

  • It says "nothing spent" without words. A drawstring pouch pulled shut is a purse nobody has opened. A gullak reads as saving, which collides with the savings entries Spends can list. A blank bill reads as a receipt you forgot.
  • It is the onboarding family. The same fist that holds up the calendar and receipt in D144 holds the potli, so Spends feels like the same hand, still helping. Concepts 2 and 3 have no hand and would look like a different set.
  • Indian, plain, not a mascot. A potli is everyday and familiar. The ₹ on its face is the only text, as on the onboarding receipt.
  • The gap is the problem. About 70% of the page is blank. Inside the card the art would grow the card but leave the same void under it. In the gap it fills the void, as onboarding fills the gap above its controls, and the card stays a quiet sentence with its link. The card keeps its height, so nothing jumps when the first spend arrives and the picture goes.

Runner-up: Concept 3, Clean bill on the spike. It is the closest to a ledger and the most Indian (a kirana bill spike), but with no hand it breaks from the onboarding set.
