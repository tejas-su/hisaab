Savings proposal

Spec §5, Phase 1, drawn. A saving is a monthly rule like a bill, but it goes in rather than out. It is never spending: it is never a bite on today's bar, never in Where it went, and it is shown with the piggy bank glyph and the word "Saved", never a colour. Settings gets a Savings row beside Fixed bills, and the list, the wizard and the paper-slip sheet are the bill screens of mockup 27 switched on the rule's kind. Put in extra is the one new screen. Today is Mon 26 Oct 2026; the ₹5,000 RD on the 10th went in on Sat 10 Oct, so today's share is ₹1,294 and the hero shows ₹874. Each frame is shown in light and dark at 360dp.

For you to decide

  • "Fixed bills" or "Bills"? Spec §5 renames the Settings row to Bills; the plan and this mockup keep Fixed bills, because Phase 1 has no changing bills yet. Drawn: Fixed bills, renamed in Phase 2.
  • The Home frame is a composite. The Saved row shows on the 10th itself, and the nudge one to five days before it, so the same RD never shows both on one day. With the RD on the 10th, "on Thu" is Thu 10 Dec 2026. The hero keeps 26 Oct's numbers, since nothing in it changes.
  • Day wording. The brief says "10th"; bills say "Every month on day 29". Drawn: "10th" on the slip row and "Every month on the 10th" on the list, both marked draft for the copy deck.
