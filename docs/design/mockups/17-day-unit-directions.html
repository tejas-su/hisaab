Day unit directions Proposal, not agreed in SPEC

The four directions from case study 12, so you can pick one. A · Dot ledger is the case study's top pick, C · Glass and road its fallback, B · Blister card third, and D · Day dial is shown only to say why not. Marigold sits only on today, with the 1.5dp displayEdge ink edge in light. Everything else is grey: state is fill, outline, hatch or checker, and words. No red, no green. Springs are critically damped (snap 250, standard 350, calm 550ms, no overshoot); the number rides the same spring as the level; a spend lands with a 3% squash. Press any card for the 0.97 sink.

D · Day dial Rejected: why

A ring of ticks around the amount, today a marigold wedge at 12 o'clock, payday a gap with the flag. One static card, no animation.

Rejected: why

  1. Rings are rejected (D7: "Apple-owned, angle reads poorly"; Cleveland and McGill, UI_DESIGN §12).
  2. 31 ticks on a circle have no start or end to count from, and week groups on a circle read as a clock face.
  3. The 76sp amount doesn't fit inside. Here it had to drop to 40sp, and at 1.5× text it breaks the ring or the number.
  4. A rotating ring is the spinner idiom ("loading"), and the new day would rotate it one tick.
  5. Clue needs colour arcs and a legend to make its cycle ring readable; Hisaab has one colour.

How to choose

Case study 12's blind read: 5 real people per round, mixed literacy, Hindi and English, UPI-first, on low-end Android. Show A, C and the current blocks cold (B too if you still like it), then ask:

  1. "How many days until payday?" Time it and count errors at 9, 28 and 31 days.
  2. "How much is left today?"
  3. "What happened?" after the over-today frame strip.
  4. A Single Ease Question (SEQ) after each task.
  5. Does A read as signal bars, a battery or a Nothing phone? Does C read as a battery or a loading bar? Does B read as a planner?

Also open: the smallest dot that holds 1.5:1 outdoors on a low-end panel (design-accessibility), and whether week groups follow the calendar or count from today.

Decided here, where case study 12 is silent

  • A's dot size is 5dp with a 2dp gap, not 4.5dp. It fits 28 days (213dp of 226) and exactly 40dp tall, and it reads less faint (see the dot-size check). 29–31 days step down to 4.5dp.
  • A and C group by 7 days counted from today, so 28 days is always four full groups. B groups by calendar week because it is a calendar.
  • A's slow down shows the true level (1.3 dots at 9 days, so one lit dot and one dim one) inside a marigold ring, not exactly one dot.
  • A's ghost dots use blockIdle, not hairline: dark hairline is 1.3:1 on the card, under the 1.5:1 floor.
  • B's caption drops "Today" (today is not at the start edge) and keeps "N days to payday". Its flag cell holds a flag and the date number only.
  • C at 1 day left has no rail: just the tank and the flag.
  • Underspend growth starts the future units at 0.85 so it shows; the true ratio is 0.98.
