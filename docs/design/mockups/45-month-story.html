Your September, in six pages

A month story for a finished cycle, opened from Spends only. It has six full-screen pages you tap through: the month, what you kept, where it went, your days, against last month, and the end. It states only what it can count. It never labels you, never moves on by itself, and nothing leaves the phone. Tap the right side of the story to go on and the left to go back, or use the arrow keys or the list.

How you get there, on Spends only

There's no notification and nothing on Home. The story belongs to a finished month, so its main door sits on that month in Spends. For the first 7 days of the new cycle, a slim row on the current month points back to it, because that's when people open Spends to look back.

Rules the story keeps

RuleWhy
Counts, never labelsPrinciple 94. Monzo's "mainly, you fast fooded" ended up with the UK Financial Ombudsman. No adjectives about the person, no ranks, and no "great" or "oh no".
No auto-advanceIndian UPI users in the research asked to turn off timers. A 5-year-old can tap, and TalkBack gets Next and Back buttons.
Spent only in the pages about spendingPages 3 to 5 leave out Saved, Used and Not logged, as BR-14 does. Bills sit on page 3 but not in the day grid on page 4.
Same numbers as SpendsBuilt from the same use cases as Where it went (BR-13) and the split bar, so the story and the screen never disagree.
Pages drop out, not fakeNo last cycle means no page 5. No income means page 2 shows the spend alone. Fewer than 10 entries means no story at all.
