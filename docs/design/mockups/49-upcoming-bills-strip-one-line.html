Next 7 days, direction C: one quiet line proposal

Home gets one 56dp sunk row and nothing else changes. The row says Next 7 days and 3 bills · ₹4,200; a tap opens a bottom sheet (the bill sheet's look, mockup 27) with every bill and saving due tomorrow to day 7, one 56dp row each; each row opens that bill's own sheet. When bills use all of today's share, the same gentle words show in both places. With one bill the row is today's due-soon row and opens that bill directly. Today is Thu 19 Nov 2026. Frames are static; the sheet rise uses mockup 27's spring and is not redrawn here.

Frames

Annotations

Alignment (HisaabSpacing.gutter)

  • The row spans the hero card's outer edges: 16dp from the screen on both sides (turn the guides on above). It is the same box as today's due-soon row, so it needs no new edge.
  • Inside the row: 8dp start padding, a 40dp well, 12dp gap, text, 12dp end padding. The dart row uses HisaabSpacing.m for the start inset so the well's glyph lands on the hero's content inset; mockup 27 draws 8dp. I kept 8dp and have not measured the glyph against the hero's 20dp inset on a device.
  • In the sheet, title, warning and list card share the sheet's 20dp edge. Rows inset 16dp; hairlines start at 68dp (16 + 40 well + 12 gap), the text start, and end 16dp from the card edge.
  • Amounts sit in a right column that ends 12dp before the chevron. There is no Flexible beside Expanded: the text column is the only flexible child.

Tap targets

  • Strip row: full width, minimum 56dp, grows with text. One target; no inner buttons.
  • Sheet rows: full card width, minimum 56dp, each row one target. No row is under 48dp at 1.5x text (frame 4).
  • The sheet closes by scrim tap, drag and system back. No separate close key, so nothing small is added.
  • Pressed state: the row steps from sunk to hairline (strip) or from surface to sunk (sheet row), as in mockup 27.

TalkBack

  • Row, normal: "Next 7 days: 3 bills, 4,200 rupees. Opens the list."
  • Row, warning: "Next 7 days: 3 bills, 4,200 rupees. Bills coming up use all of today's share. Opens the list." The glyph is decorative; the words carry the state.
  • One bill: "Electricity, about 1,200 rupees, goes out tomorrow. Opens the bill."
  • Sheet: a modal; focus lands on the title, then the warning, then rows. Row: "Electricity, about 1,200 rupees, tomorrow. Opens the bill." Card: "HDFC card, 8,500 rupees, pay by Thursday. Not in the total. Opens the bill." Labels are unique because names are.
  • Closing the sheet returns focus to the row.

Contrast (WCAG ratios computed from the hex values)

  • Light: ink2 on sunk 5.55:1; ink2 on surface 7.20:1; ink2 on pressed hairline 4.71:1; ink on sunk 13.84:1.
  • Dark: ink2 on sunk 5.48:1; ink2 on surface 6.42:1; ink2 on pressed hairline 4.68:1; ink on sunk 12.41:1.
  • The warning outline is ink2 on bg 6.14:1 (light), above the 3:1 for UI parts. The warning words are ink, not ink2, so the one line that matters is the strongest on the row.
  • No marigold, red or green. State is words, a glyph, an outline and a fill.

Honest pros and cons against a plain list

  • Pro: least height: 56dp on Home, the same as today's row, so the hero and Day Blocks stay first and second and the Today list loses nothing. Nothing moves when bills change.
  • Pro: the warning is visible without opening anything, and the sheet repeats it where the bills are.
  • Pro: with one bill, which is common, no slower than today.
  • Con: it costs a tap to see the bills. With 2 or more bills Home shows only a count and a total, not which bill.
  • Con: the sheet is a second layer to learn, and the plan's "strip lists each bill on Home" becomes "strip points to a list". The plan's strip rows would be dropped from Home.
  • Con: a count well tells less at a glance than the bill's own icon.

Open questions for the owner

  • 1. Is one tap to see the bills acceptable for 2 or more bills, or must the soonest bill's name show on Home (for example, "Electricity and 2 more")?
  • 2. In the sheet, should a card bill sit in date order among the others (drawn) or after the counted bills, so the total's rows stay together?
  • 3. The total counts changing bills at their estimate, as the pool does. Should the row say "about ₹4,200" when any counted bill is changing? Drawn: no, only the row for that bill says "about".
