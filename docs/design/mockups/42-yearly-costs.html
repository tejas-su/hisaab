Yearly costs, and Used proposal

A yearly or term cost (insurance, school fees, Diwali) is set up as a monthly saving: three new name chips and one caption on the existing Add a saving flow, no new question, field or setting. When the cost falls due the user opens the saving and taps Used some of it, so the pot does not read falsely high. A Used entry is in no BR-3 symbol: it never reaches the hero, the bar, the Today total or the Spends bars. Worked example: Insurance ₹1,000 a month on the 5th, ₹12,000 set aside, today Mon 26 Oct with ₹420 spent, hero ₹1,318 before and after. Every frame is drawn in light and dark at 360dp; frames at 320dp carry text at 1.5×.

Colour use: marigold appears only on the Next and Save keys (the act) and today's bar. Used some of it is an outlined key on purpose: it is the second-most important thing on the slip, and a second marigold would shout. No red, no green; a refused digit is a word, a haptic and an outline flash.

Problems found in the docs

  1. 1. The 320 × 568 sheet does not fit with a 64dp keypad and a wide Save. 04 section 9 and the deck say 64dp keys; mockups 27, 28 and 40 and the built keypad use 56dp. At 56dp the Used sheet needs about 16 (handle) + 56 (title row) + 118 (compact slip with a three-line slot) + 8 + 4 × 56 + 3 × 8 (keys) + 68 (Save) + 20 = about 534dp of 568 minus the 28dp status bar, so it fits at 1× with nothing to spare. With 64dp keys it needs 566dp and does not. At 1.5× the body scrolls above the fixed keypad as the deck says (section 9 shows it), but then the title (3 lines) has to scroll off for the amount and slot to show at all (section 9, first frame). That frame is the empty state; the refusal state is a three-line reason plus the two-line fixed line, which the keypad cuts. All of it fits beside ₹0 but wraps below a six-digit amount. Fix: at large text, drop the Back well's title to the system title row (one line, "Used from Insurance") and keep the amount, chip and slot in the fixed part; or give the slot priority over the title. Fix: keep 56dp keys here; or move the action key into the keypad's bottom-right cell as 04's wireframe has it, which saves the 68dp Save row. That wireframe has no 00 key, and 04 also says "shared Keypad", so the two cannot both be true. Pick one; I drew the existing shared keypad with 00 and a wide Save.
  2. 2. The Savings list row has three things to say and room for two. The deck gives the row's second line to "{amount} set aside", but the existing row's second line is "Every month on the 5th", and the UX doc's S6 shows both. Drawn: the schedule stays as line 2 and "₹12,000 set aside" is a third line (only above ₹0), so the row grows from 64 to 84dp, and at 320dp with 1.5× text the trailing ₹1,000 leaves a text column of about 110dp, so the three lines become six (section 9). That is an existing weakness of any row with a trailing amount, but this feature adds the third line. Alternative: join them with a middle dot, which wraps unpredictably at 360dp. Recommendation: the third line.
  3. 3. "Insurance · Used from savings" on one line does not fit a Today row. The title column at 360dp is about 176dp, and the string is about 220dp. The deck's own note says the row wraps to two lines. Drawn as name over Used from savings, matching the Saved row, which needs the deck's entryUsed to carry the kind word alone and the saving's name to come from the row. Needs a decision from design-content (the spoken form "Insurance, used from savings, 4,000 rupees" is unaffected).
  4. 4. The slot's refusal copy is not the UX doc's. UX doc: "That's all that's set aside. Add anything more as a spend." Deck: "That's all you have set aside. Add the rest as a spend." Used the deck's (verbatim, as asked). The deck's caption ("divided by 12") also replaces the UX doc's "about a twelfth", and "₹12,000 set aside" replaces "set aside so far". The brief asked for "so far"; the deck has no such string, so I drew the deck. Four strings in the UX doc and 03 need the same update.
  5. 5. "Used some of it" and the hint repeat each other. On the slip the hint ("Paid the cost from this? Tap Used some of it.") sits directly above a key that says Used some of it. Drawn as written. If the blind read (B8) shows the key is understood alone, drop the hint and gain 40dp on short windows. On an RD "the cost" reads oddly (open question 4 in the deck); the chips-only option is cheap, since the hint is already conditional on a balance.
  6. 6. The caption plus hint is five lines at 1.5× on the amount step. The step has a fixed keypad, so the body scrolls and, scrolled to the end, the amount being typed is off screen (section 9, third frame). The hint is the discovery cue for Used, so it is the sentence a short window would drop first; the deck allows that. Better: at large text show the caption above the slip and keep the amount in the fixed part, or drop the hint there (the sheet's own hint carries discovery too).
  7. 7. Snackbar wording on All of it. "Insurance: ₹12,000 used" does not say that the daily number is unchanged, and Undo restores the pot. Acceptable (the keypad already promised it), but it is the one place the doc's "peak-end moment" has no reassurance. Not changed.
