Bills that change, and the card bill proposal

Spec §5, Phase 2, drawn. A bill whose amount changes (electricity) is never counted by itself: on its day it waits, Home asks "How much?" in the nudge slot, and one tap on Save confirms last month's amount. A credit card bill is recorded but never counted, because every swipe already was; its row says so in words, with the card glyph and no colour. Today is Mon 26 Oct 2026. Electricity is due today and still waiting, so it stays set aside at its ₹1,200 estimate and the hero is the worked example's ₹1,430, with 9 days to payday. Each frame is shown in light and dark at 360dp. Turn on Show edges to check the shared gutters.

For you to decide

  • The Amount changes? value is too long for BillAnswerRow. As built, the value is one line capped at 38% of the row (about 106dp at 360dp), so "Changes every month" would read "Changes ev…". Drawn: two lines, end-aligned, leader on the last line (the row grows from 56 to 64dp). The other way is a shorter value ("Changes", "Same", "Card") with the full words on the chips; that is the content designer's call. Either needs maxLines: 2 or new strings before Task 5.
  • Picked chip in a full-width stack. HisaabChip(expand: true) centres its label and check together. Drawn: label at the start, check on the end edge, so the three labels share one edge. That is a change in design-system's chip.
  • The slip of a waiting bill. The eyebrow says Fixed bill today, which a changing bill isn't; drawn Bill, as the copy deck proposes. "Counted 26 Oct" would be wrong while it waits, and "Next goes out on 26 Nov" too early, so frame 4 draws no stamp and no footer. A stamp such as Waiting needs copy.
  • The question's title. Drawn as the row's own words, Amount changes?, so no new string. A full question ("Does Electricity change each month?") would need one.
  • The Bills page hint said "They go out by themselves on their day", which is untrue for a changing bill. Drawn with the copy deck's proposal: "Most go out by themselves on their day. If the amount changes, Home asks how much."
  • Composite. The worked example's Electricity is due on the 29th; here it is due on the 26th (today) so frames 1, 2 and 4 show one bill. The hero is the same either way, because a waiting bill stays in B at its estimate (spec example 2).
