Month story art: three versions

These are line patterns for the Your month card and the six story pages, in the style of the hero card's banknote etch and with Spotify Wrapped as the reference. All three are monochrome: no new colour, no category colours, and marigold stays on the one act key at the end. Each page has its own motif: rosette (the month), ripples (kept), sunburst (where it went), waves (your days), two orbits (against last month) and day-block confetti (the end). Scroll each row sideways.

1 · Quiet etch

closest to the hero

Thin 1dp lines in hairline over a faint grain, at the same strength as the hero's rosette. It feels premium and calm, but it reads as texture rather than as an event. In the app, each page's art would settle in once (a slight turn and fade) behind the words.

2 · Bold art

my pick

The same motifs, larger and brighter: lines step up to a mid-grey (still under 3:1 on the page, so the words always win), they fill about half of each page and some shapes are solid. It's the closest to Wrapped's energy without adding a hue. The card gets the rosette, grain and the hero's double keyline.

3 · Poster

most Wrapped

Bold art plus the page's key number set huge in outline type, cropped off the bottom edge like a poster. It's the loudest version. Watch for: the outline number repeats what the page already says (it's hidden from TalkBack), and at 2× text it needs to shrink or drop.
