The entry card: four versions with motion

Round 3 of case study 38. Only direction A is left: tap a row and it opens into a card where it is. Each phone below works. Tap the Food ₹250 row. Use the switches to slow the motion down, see it with reduced motion, and see where a haptic fires.

A1 · Card with sheets, polished closest to what you picked

The card you liked, with the note typed in place. Amount, category and day still open a sheet from the bottom.

  • The row grows into the card (standard spring) while the rest of the list fades back and the hero tucks into one line. That gives the card room and shows Home's number is on hold until you're done (D91).
  • The ▾ turns over when its sheet opens.
  • Each saved part draws a small tick beside it, which fades after 1.6 s. A changed amount rolls to its new value, and a new category's icon slides in.
  • Done folds the card back into the row, which outlines once. Then the hero opens up, its number rolls, and the bar moves, with the success haptic on that same frame.

A2 · Everything inside the card no sheets at all

Your note idea, applied to everything. Nothing opens over the card: each part unfolds inside it, one at a time.

  • Amount: a compact keypad unfolds under it, and the old amount turns picked. Your first digit makes the old number lift away as the new one comes in. The key says what will happen: "Keep ₹250", then "Change to ₹350".
  • Category: a 4×2 grid unfolds with Food outlined. Tap Travel, the grid folds and the chip's icon slides to the new one.
  • Day: a row of day pills unfolds.
  • Moving on saves: tapping another part, Done or the note keeps what you typed, just like the note. One Undo at the end reverses it all.

A2 + New · Make a category without leaving your ask

A2 with the dashed New tile after your own, as in Add (D138). Tap Food ▾, then New, and the grid folds into a naming step inside the card. The keyboard comes up and the card rises above it.

  • Name: type, or tap a chip (Groceries, Family, Pets, Salon…). The chips narrow as you type and leave out names you already have.
  • Icon: picked from the name ("Icon from the name: Bag"). Change unfolds 8 icons in the card, not a new screen.
  • One key: "Move to Groceries" makes the category and moves the entry into it. The grid folds and the chip's icon slides in, as with any other pick.
  • Already have it? Type "food" and it says "You have Food already. It moves there."
  • Room: after your 4th own category the New tile goes, and one line says how to make room, as in Add.
  • ‹ Categories goes back to the grid with nothing made. The note and Done wait underneath while you name.

A2 in phone landscape two panes, no dock

A phone on its side (and a tablet, or an unfolded foldable in landscape) is expanded: Home on the left beside Spends, no dock, and the Add key at the bottom of the Home pane (§13). The card stays in its list, and the other pane becomes a board with every part at once: days on top, the keypad on the left, your categories (your own and New included) on the right, and a live line, "Today's share ₹430 → ₹330". Nothing has to be opened first. Tap the Food row, then just type or tap a tile.

  • The Add key slides away while the card is open and comes back on Done, like the dock.
  • Move category: row → tile → Done. Fix the amount: row → digits → Done (the first digit replaces, and the card shows the digits as you type).
  • Tapping ₹250, Food ▾ or Today ▾ in the card outlines that part of the board for a moment, so the card and the board read as one.
  • New swaps the categories for the naming step, in the same spot; the keypad and days stay. The note is still typed in the card.
  • An entry opened from Every entry (in the Spends pane) uses the mirror rule: the question opens in the pane that isn't holding the card.

A3 · Inside the card, with "was" see and put back

A2, plus a trail. Each part you change leaves a small ₹250 Put back under the card. You can see exactly what changed before you leave, and undo one part without undoing the rest.

  • Useful when a fix touches two things (amount and category) and you only meant one.
  • It reassures people who worry about "breaking" their numbers. The snackbar Undo still covers everything after Done.
  • Cost: one more line in the card, only after a change.

A4 · Tap where it's wrong fewest taps

A2, but the row itself has three tap zones. Tap the amount in the row and the card opens with the keypad already out. Tap the icon and it opens with the categories out. Tap anywhere else and it opens plain. Moving to Travel becomes row icon → Travel → Done.

  • It teaches itself once: the first time a card opens, a dotted line draws under each part you can tap (amount, Food, Today, note), one after another, and the ▾s nudge once. After that it stays quiet. With reduced motion the lines just appear.
  • The zones are a shortcut, not a secret. Tapping anywhere on the row still works, so nobody has to know about them.
  • TalkBack gets three actions on the row: "Change amount", "Change category" and "Open".
