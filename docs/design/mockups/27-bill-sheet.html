Bill sheet proposal

Case study 16, drawn, round 2. Tap a bill (from Home's due-soon line or from Settings › Fixed bills) and a sheet rises holding the bill itself: a paper slip with a torn edge, a perforation and a stamp. Each answer has its own row, and tapping one opens just that question and saves it alone. This round: the stamp sits on the amount line, Delete is a full-width key where Save sits, and every bill gets an icon, picked from its name, which you can change. Today is Mon 26 Oct 2026.

Try it

  1. Tap Electricity ₹1,200 goes out in 3 days. The sheet rises and the stamp lands beside the amount.
  2. Tap Name and type Airtel fibre, Gas cylinder or Netflix. The icon on the slip follows as you type. Press Save.
  3. Tap Icon and pick another one. From then on, renaming keeps your pick. Picking the one the name suggests (the tile with "From the name" under it) goes back to following the name.
  4. Tap Amount, type 1350 and press Save. The first key replaces the old amount.
  5. Tap Day of the month. Pick 20 and D34 moves it to Fri 20 Nov, so the stamp goes and the due line leaves Home.
  6. Delete bill closes the sheet and offers Undo. Close with the scrim or Esc.

Every screen

Bill icons

Sixteen glyphs, all in the free Hugeicons stroke-rounded set, each with a label (icons always carry one). The name picks the icon on the fly: the first group whose word appears in the name wins, and the groups are checked top to bottom, so "Car EMI" is a loan and "Airtel fibre" is internet. With no match, the bill keeps today's Bill glyph. The six onboarding pills map straight across: Rent → Home, EMI → Loan, Phone → Phone, Internet → Internet, School fees → School, Subscriptions → TV & apps.

Notes for the build

  • Data: recurring_rules gains icon TEXT NULL in a migration. Null means "follow the name". A pure BillIcon.forName(String) in the ledger domain holds the word table, written test-first: "Car EMI" → loan, "Airtel fibre" → internet, "Light bill" → power, no match → bill, case and spaces ignored.
  • Where the icon shows: the slip, the Fixed bills rows (today all use AppIcons.recurring), Home's due-soon row and the Add wizard's name step (live, as here). Entries posted by a bill keep their category's icon; the bill's icon is the bill's alone.
  • No colour: icons are ink in sunk wells. Telling bills apart comes from shape, not hue (no category colours).
  • TalkBack: the slip reads "Electricity, power, ₹1,200, every month on day 29, due in 3 days". Rows read "Change icon, Power". Tiles read "Power, picked, from the name".
  • Reuse: the amount, day and name questions are today's step widgets with a Save label. The icon question is new (BillIconStep, a 4×4 grid like the category tiles). The slip is a new shared BillSlip.

For you to decide

  • Decided: "Counted", not "Paid". Nobody marks a bill as paid. The engine counts it on its day (BR-9) and can't know the money left, so the stamp says what the app did. "Count it today" for early payers is held until someone reports the gap.
  • Hindi and regional words in the table (bijli, kiraya, doodh, bai)? Users type names in Latin script, and these are cheap to add. Drawn: included.
  • Sixteen icons enough? More means a scrolling grid. I'd rather keep 16 and let Bill cover the rest.
