What to take from Unfold proposal

Case study 14's seven "Take" items. Each one shows the screen as built next to the proposal, in hisaab's own tokens: one signal colour, no red or green, and state shown by fill, hatch and words. Unfold's own screens aren't redrawn here. Copy marked draft needs a design-content pass.

R1 + R2 · Limit rows in words, with a key for the hatch recommended

Today a row with a limit shows its share (%) until it goes over, and the hatch is never explained. Proposed: a row with a limit says "₹200 left", "₹0 left" or "₹840 past limit", and one key line appears under the card only when a row is hatched. Cost: two ARB strings and a branch in category_spend_row.dart.

R6 · This week: a bar per weekday to test

Tapping the hero shows This week (FR-HO-2). Today the number changes and the Day Blocks stay. Proposed (case study 15 §2): seven bars, Mon to Sun, in the Day Blocks' own 40dp row, so the hero keeps its height. Each past bar has its own share tick (that day's share as it stood, the same tick as Where it went), and the part above it is hatched. Today is marigold with its own tick. Future days are idle stubs. The letters replace the captions, and the range slot says the week in words, so no key is needed. Shares are illustrative.

R6b · Giving the bars room owner's question

The bars above feel tight at 40dp. Three ways to give them room, each on a whole 360 × 780 phone (shown at 80%) with Home's Today list and the dock. Tap a hero to toggle Today ↔ This week. The dashed line shows where the Today list starts, measured live. W1 is the 4:5 card. W2 grows the hero only in This week, on your tap. W3 makes the row taller in both modes.

R5 · A small, raised ₹ on the hero to test

The ₹ drops to about 42% of the digit size and sits at the cap line, so the digits carry the weight. Hero only. TalkBack still reads "1,430 rupees". Check it at 1.5× text (E12).

R4 · Hide figures from the recent-apps screen recommended

No UI. On Android 13+ the app switcher stops capturing hisaab, so it shows the system's placeholder instead of your money, and screenshots keep working (case study 15 §3). Android 7–12: no change in v1, because FLAG_SECURE would block screenshots and toggling it on pause is unreliable. The visible eye button is shown last, as an option that needs a blind read. Its state would persist across launches.

R7 · Search notes in Every entry dropped (D113)

A field at the top of Every entry that matches notes and category names. It sits on the list screen, so the Add flow is untouched. Type swiggy, ravi or auto. This is lower value while notes stay optional, so revisit it with usage data or with M6.

R3 · Yesterday on the date chip already built

Case study 14 assumed Today ▾ goes straight to a calendar. It doesn't: date_chip.dart already opens Today · Yesterday · Pick a date, so back-dating to yesterday takes 2 taps. Nothing to do, and the case study should be corrected.

Store screenshots handoff to design-brand

Google Play's rules (case study 15 §5): taglines take at most 20% of the image, the first three screenshots lead with UI, there's no device imagery, and there are no "Best", "#1", "New" or "Free". So each frame is the real app, full-bleed, under a short tagline band. Light comes first and dark last. The taglines are drafts.
