Dot ledger options Proposal, not agreed in SPEC

Direction A from mockup 17, the dot ledger, worked on the two worries: what one dot means and how you tell dates. Section 1 compares four meanings, section 2 four ways to show dates, and section 3 builds three full versions with every state and every motion. Marigold sits only on today, inside the 1.5dp displayEdge ink edge in light. Everything else is grey: state is fill, outline, hatch and words. No red, no green. The whole card is one tap target (press for the 0.97 sink); TalkBack reads one sentence and nothing per dot.

Numbers. A = ₹1,850 a day, ₹420 spent, so today shows ₹1,430. Payday moves to Sat 7 Nov (salary by the 7th) so "today" is a real Thursday: Thu 29 Oct at 9 days, Thu 22 Oct at 16 and Sat 10 Oct at 28. The first week is part of a week, and the cycle crosses into November. Fixed from 17: dots are 5dp with a 2dp gap (never smaller in M1), over today is outlined empty dots over a hatch (no checker), and the page has no sideways scroll at 360dp.

1 · What is one dot?

Each option on track at 9 and 28 days, and over today at 9 days (₹1,020 over, so each day after gets ₹1,722). All use D1 dates. Replay shows a ₹300 spend.

2 · How do you tell the dates?

All on M1, on track at 9, 16 and 28 days. The question for each: "When is payday? What date is next Monday?" in 2 seconds.

3 · Improved versions

Three full combinations. Every state at 9 and 28 days (and 1 day left), then six motions, each with its own Replay. Springs are critically damped (snap 250, standard 350, calm 550ms, no overshoot); the number rides the same spring as the dots; the last dot out lands with a 3% squash. With Reduce motion on, every replay is a 150ms crossfade to the end state.

Can a 5-year-old say what a dot is? Not "a sixth", but yes to "the yellow dots are today's money; buying puts them out". Every column is a day, so "how many days" is "how many columns". One meaning per dot. Half. Today's big dots are money; the small dots are whole days. A dot means two things, and a child asked "what is a dot?" gets two answers. As V1. The glow makes today read as "lit", which helps. M2: "a dot is ₹500" is a number to learn, and it changes with income. M4: "it's loading".
Payday date and next Monday's date in 2 seconds? Payday: yes, the flag reads "7 Nov". Next Monday: yes, it is the first, bold label ("Mon 2"). Counting days at 28 is slower than in 17 (calendar weeks give 2 + 7 + 7 + 7 + 5). Same labels as V1, so yes and yes. As V1. Same D1 labels; M4's ring pushes them right.
Legible at 28 days? Yes: 5dp dots, 2dp gaps, 2.2dp at 28 days with a 6dp week gap. Over today is now holes over a hatch inside an 11dp capsule: the empty column reads, the hatch shows as a texture only in its 3dp margin and gaps (check outdoors). Yes, the quietest row. Today's 9dp dots read from far away. But 27 single dots need as much width as 27 columns (no width saved), and at 30 days they step down to 4.5dp. Yes. The displayEtch ring inside a 5dp dot is only visible up close (it is a texture, not a signal). M2 overflows at a fine step. M4 at 28 drops the later dots to 4dp, below the floor.

Open questions Is one dot (₹308 here) too coarse? Should later days lose a whole dot for a 2% cut (28 days)? At 31 days with 5 Monday breaks the row is 242dp of 242: the flag date must stay short. Are dates above the row (not under, see D1) OK? Does the two-meaning dot confuse in a blind read? Does the 2 × 3 die read as "dice" (a game)? Do the two new marigolds pass the colour process (OKLCH, contrast against blockIdle, mockup compare)? Is the odometer worth its cost on a low-end phone? None: M2 fails the ₹300 test and M4 reads as loading.

Ranking: V1, then V3 (V1 with a finish you can add later without changing meaning), then V2. Blind-read V1 against V2 with 5 people (case study 12's script): "what is one dot?", "how many days to payday?", "what date is next Monday?".
