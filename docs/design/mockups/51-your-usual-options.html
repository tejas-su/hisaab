Your usual: five ways to show it

Decided: option A (D207). The app builds it at 40dp in the same 48dp slot, with a 32dp well and cap, so the 12dp gap to the chips holds.

These are proposals for the your usual offer on step 1 of the Add tray. 0 is what shipped before D207. A to D are new options. All of them run the same example: Monday, 10:05 am, with a Chai ₹20 habit. Use the "Two habits" switch to add a Travel ₹60 habit at the same time. Type on the keypad to see the offer give way to the preview. Tap the offer to see it save, close the tray and show Undo.

Each option keeps the same rules. It's one tap and adds no step to Add → digits → Next → tile. The keypad, dock and category tiles stay where they are. Marigold stays on Next only. There's no colour, and state is shown by fill and words.

Recommendation

Go with A. It fixes the real problem (the pill doesn't look tappable) without moving anything or changing any rule. It also takes away the one outline exception in a fill-only design. If people still miss it after A, B is the next step, but it needs a decision to let the tray grow when an offer is there. D can be added on top of A later, once we know overlapping habits are common.
