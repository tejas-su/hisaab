Your usual approved, D153

Case study 32, drawn, from the design in docs/features/habits/. When you open Add at the time you usually log the same spend, the empty line under ₹0 offers it: Chai ₹20 · your usual. One tap adds it and closes the tray, with Undo. Type anything and the pill gives way to the usual preview. Nothing moves, nothing new appears on the normal path, no notification is sent, and the suggestion is worked out on the phone. Today is Mon 26 Oct 2026; ₹420 is spent and ₹1,430 is safe.

Approved (D153): BR-17, FR-AD-10 and FR-SE-2 AC-6. The preview line becomes a fixed 48dp slot in layout B (FR-AD-1, UI_DESIGN §5.3), and a one-tap add is the only save with an Undo (BR-10). Phases 2 and 3 below are still proposals.

Try it

Drag the clock. Weekdays have two habits: Chai ₹20 around 10 am and Food ₹120 around 1 pm. Weekends have none yet. Press Add on Home: inside a window the pill is there, outside it the line is empty. Tap the pill, then Undo. Leave chai unlogged and move the clock past 12:15 to see Home ask.

1. How it learns

The last four weeks of chai, one row per day. Each dot is a Chai entry logged the same day it was spent. Back-dated entries, bills and edits that moved the date are left out. The band is the window: the half-hour slots above half the peak, plus 15 minutes either side. The amount is the one used most, here ₹20 in 17 of 18 hits (94%).

2. Add, step 1: the right one at the right time

The pill sits in the line that is empty at ₹0 today, so nothing above or below it moves when it comes or goes. That line becomes a fixed 48dp slot for everyone. Layout B pays for it by tightening the gaps: 20dp from Spent/Got to the amount (was 30), 4dp to the slot, 8dp to the chips (was 16). The keypad sits only 8dp lower, once, and the 40dp pill keeps 12dp clear of the chips, so a thumb on +₹20 doesn't add chai. The pill is outlined in ink2 on surface with no fill and no marigold: it is an offer, not the action. Next stays the one marigold key. At most one pill; when two habits overlap, the one with the higher daily chance wins.

3. Tap it, or type

Type anything (a digit, a chip, ⌫, or switching to Got) and the pill crossfades to the preview line in 150ms with no height change. It doesn't come back until the tray opens again. Tap the pill and it fills ink for 120ms, then the usual close runs: tray drops, today's bar drains, the number rolls, success haptic. A snackbar says what happened, "Added ₹20 to Chai", with Undo for 4 s (kept until dismissed under TalkBack). Undo also quiets that habit for the day. Because the pill only repeats an amount you logged, it never suggests spending more.

4. Big text, and every way in

At 1.5× text the pill drops "· your usual" and grows to 48dp; if "Chai ₹20" still doesn't fit one line, it isn't shown. The app shortcut and Quick Settings tile (D147) open this same tray, so they get the pill too. That's the moment someone is most likely adding a routine spend.

5. Home asks once, later phase 2

90 minutes after the window ends with nothing in Chai today, Home's one note line asks. It only shows if no higher note is waiting (over today, a bill due, "you saved"), and at most once a day. It's a question, so "no" is a fine answer: × hides it for today, and two ×'s in 14 days rest it for 30 days. Tapping opens Add with the pill carried, whatever the time.

6. See it, forget it new from the research

The one place the app says what it noticed is where you already manage the category: Settings › Categories › Chai. One row, "Your usual", shown only once the habit is Regular. Forget clears it and rests it for 30 days, with Undo. There's no new screen and no on/off setting. The research found that accurate guesses with no explanation feel creepy, and that people trust a suggestion more when they can control it.

7. Bills hiding in plain sight phase 3

The same engine, on a monthly rhythm: the same note and amount in 3 cycles in a row, near the same day. It joins Spends' insights within the cap of 3. Make it a fixed bill opens the bill flow already filled in, and you confirm the day. Nothing is created silently.
