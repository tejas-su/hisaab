Day Blocks finishes Proposal, not agreed in SPEC

The blocks as built, with the same count, widths, gaps, heights and radius 8; only the material changes, and each finish gets one signature moment. The aim is calm and premium: a watch dial, not a toy. Marigold sits only on today, inside the 1.5dp ink displayEdge in light. Nothing is red or green. Springs are critically damped; the two settles that dip once (5 and 6) bend D44 and are marked. Each card opens in its on-track look. Replay plays its moment; with Reduce motion on, the same Replay is a 150ms crossfade to the same end state. Press a card for the 0.97 sink.
