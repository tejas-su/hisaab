Saving target: Saving up to proposal, not built

A saving gets one optional number, Saving up to, set from a last row in its sheet. The Savings list's third line then says how far along it is in words (done by May 2027, reached ₹12,000), and Home says "Insurance reached ₹12,000." once, in the existing nudge slot. Nothing else on Home, in Add or in the hero changes: the hero is ₹1,318 in every frame, because a target moves no BR-3 symbol. Every frame is drawn at 360dp in light and dark; the 320dp frames carry text at 1.5×.

Changed after D213 (owner, 2026-11-23): a saving now stops putting in when what is set aside reaches the target. (1) The saving's sheet, when reached, shows two sunk keys under the set-aside line, I bought it and Keep saving; closing the sheet leaves it as it is (frame 6). (2) I bought it closes the sheet and the Savings list shows the snackbar "Used ₹50,000 from RD" with Undo, 4 s; Undo restores the saving and removes the Used entry (frame 7). (3) Keep saving clears the target, the row reads as a plain saving, and the footer reads "Changed." (frame 8). (4) The "Put in 10 Oct" stamp and the "Next goes in" foot are not drawn on a stopped saving; no copy says it keeps putting in. The Home line "Insurance reached ₹12,000." is unchanged. The hero stays ₹1,318 in every frame (Used moves no BR-3 symbol).

Colour use: marigold is only on the Save key (the act), today's bar and the Add key, as everywhere. The reached line, the Clear chip, the new row and the I bought it and Keep saving keys use sunk, hairline, ink and ink2 only. No red, no green, no progress bar: the words carry the state.

The sheet row's value never says "reached" or the dateThe slip's set-aside line above it does (AC-T6). One place for the estimate, so the two cannot drift.
