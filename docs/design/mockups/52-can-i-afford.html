Can I afford it?

Proposal, not built. You're looking at a big buy, say a Mac at ₹1,27,000. hisaab gives one answer in one sentence: buy it now from savings, buy it with the card and pay the full bill next month, or save up for it from salary. The maths stays hidden. The other paths are one tap away under Other ways.

The rules agreed so far: no loans and no EMIs, and the card is always paid in full. Savings are used first. Next month must keep a breathing space of your usual daily spend plus 20% (that's your floor). The same floor caps how much the app asks you to save each month. Only the money you say you've saved for this counts. Pots like Insurance are never touched.

1 · The flow

Your income is ₹45,000. Fixed bills are ₹13,000. Your usual spend is ₹750 a day, so the floor is ₹900. You've already saved ₹60,000 for the Mac.

2 · The three answers

The same Mac, with three different situations. Each answer is one sentence, plus a bar showing what next month (or today) looks like against your floor.

3 · Try it

Move the sliders and the answer updates live. The presets jump to each case.

4 · After "Start saving"

A goal is an ordinary saving rule with a target. It takes money out of the daily number like any saving and shows progress in Settings › Savings. On the day you buy it, Used some of it (BR-19) spends the pot, so the hero doesn't jump.

Open questions

  • Where it lives. The proposal puts a card on Spends next to Ask AI, with an optional app-icon shortcut. Settings › Savings shows the goals.
  • No history yet. With less than one full cycle, "usual a day" is a guess. The proposal leaves the card path out and says "Ask again after payday" rather than inventing a number.
  • Weekly and "It changes" cycles. "Next month" becomes "next week" or "next month" by cycle (D117). A weekly cycle hardly ever has card room for a big buy.
  • Goal reached. When the pot reaches the target, does the rule stop by itself or keep going? The proposal: it stops, and Home's nudge slot says "Mac: you have ₹1,27,000 set aside."
  • Docs touched. A new BR (affordability), an FR for the sheet, a target column on saving rules (a schema bump), FR-SE-1 (goal row), FR-WG (shortcut), ARB copy and a DECISIONS row.
