# Design research

UX/UI research for Hisaab: case studies of other apps, the principles we drew from them, and where to look. This folder records why a design was chosen. What was decided lives in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, and the resulting spec in `docs/UI_DESIGN.md`.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [principles.md](principles.md) | Design principles learned from research, each with its source and the Hisaab screens it applies to |
| [sources.md](sources.md) | Where to look (Apple HIG, Material, Mobbin, Dribbble, Pinterest, NN/g, pattern libraries), what each is good for, and access limits |
| [case-studies/](case-studies/) | One study per design question, numbered `NN-topic.md` |

## Case studies
| # | Topic | Outcome |

| 18 | [Bills that change every month, and fast entry](case-studies/18-variable-bills-and-quick-entry.md) | Desk research (Actual, YNAB, Chronicle, Simplifi, Monarch, Money Manager, CRED, Android alarms and notification permission, dontkillmyapp). Proposed: E, fixed bills post and changing bills wait for their amount with an estimate reserved; the card bill as a recorded, uncounted reminder; a Home "How much?" line now and a due-day notification next. Approved in the savings-and-bills spec (2026-11-04). Built: `R_t`, bills out of the pool on their day (D119); changing bills that wait with their estimate reserved, the card bill recorded and never counted, the Home "How much?" line and the bill amount sheet (D123–D125). Built in Phase 3 (D126): the due-day notification, asked for once, armed inexactly at about 09:00, a tap opening the bill amount sheet |
| 17 | [Savings and the RD](case-studies/17-savings-and-rd.md) | Desk research (Simple, Simplifi, PocketGuard, Copilot, Monzo Pots, Jupiter, Fi, Groww, SBI and HDFC RD terms, Soman & Cheema 2011). Built: B, savings that work like fixed bills, `K` computed, *Put in extra*, "Spent · Saved" on Spends (D120), and the morning note reworded (D121). Rejected: a visible keep-aside row; pots with targets |
| 16 | [Changing a fixed bill](case-studies/16-bill-edit-flow.md) | Desk research (check-answers Change links, wizard-for-create vs form-for-edit, Rocket Money, Simplifi, ZeroSum, our own entry-edit tray). Proposed: D, a bill sheet over the list: a summary with a Change row per answer (amount, day, name), each saved on its own; Delete on the summary; Add keeps the three-question wizard. Changing the amount drops from 4 taps and 4 screen changes to 3 taps in one sheet. Home's due-soon line opens the sheet; icons from the name; "Counted" stamp, no mark-as-paid. Approved (D118) |
| 15 | [Unfold takeaways: research and design plan](case-studies/15-unfold-takeaways-plan.md) | Desk research (Apple HIG Charts, Google Play's preview-asset rules, Android's `setRecentsScreenshotEnabled`, balance-hiding apps, NN/g search) and a plan covering colour, components, motion, haptics, copy, accessibility, edge cases, spec changes and tests for R1+R2 (limit rows in words, hatch key), R6 (week bars with a share tick per day, in the Day Blocks' row), R4 (no recents preview on API 33+; eye button only after a blind read), R7 (note and amount search) and store screenshots (no device frames, taglines ≤ 20%). Built (D109–D111): R4, R1+R2, R6 as W2 (the card grows 48dp only in This week, chosen over a 4:5 card). R7 search was built, then dropped by the owner (D113). Waiting: store screenshots (wordmark decision), R4b (blind read) |
| 14 | [Unfold: a close competitor, compared](case-studies/14-unfold-money.md) | Desk review of the Play listing and 6 screenshots (10+ downloads, so not validated). Confirms our calm-card, black-pill-pick, hatch-for-over and daily-sentence choices. Proposed: a words key under every hatch, "₹X left" on limit rows, *Yesterday* on the date chip, a blank recent-apps card, a small raised ₹ on the hero, and a weekday-bar *This week* picture. Rejected: spent-as-hero, red/green/category tints, the six-row Add card, accounts and net worth. Risk: its "unfold." wordmark uses the same dot device as "hisaab•" |
| 12 | [The day unit: real-world references and four new directions](case-studies/12-day-unit-references.md) | Desk research, 18 references (Nothing Glyph Matrix, LM3914, Apple discrete gauges, Android `ProgressStyle`, Flighty, calendar blister packs and others). Proposed, top pick: A, a dot ledger (a 6-dot LED column per day, grouped by week, today's dots go out one by one). Fallback: C, today's tank plus a journey rail. Third: B, a week-row blister card (too tall). Rejected: D, a day dial (rings, D7). Conflicts with D7, D91, D41. Next: a 5-user blind read |
| 11 | [Attention and memorable moments: peaks, ends and the experience curve](case-studies/11-attention-and-memorable-moments.md) | Desk research. The blog's "start low, spike, smooth end" curve is a simplification: peaks and ends dominate memory, and nothing says to start low. Proposed: the first open of the day is the daily moment, and later opens stay quiet (conflicts with D91, D106); a kind close-then-open on payday morning; end the overspend moment on a stable fact. Rejected: streaks, open loops, variable rewards. Next: a diary study across a payday |
| 10 | [The hero card: emphasis, material and proportion](case-studies/10-hero-card.md) | Built: B, "etched paper" (D106): the only raised card, a guilloché etch, grain, a sheen as the hero appears, a press. Built too: a note border (D107). Proposed in the same family: brushed metal, embossed amount, etched mark. Not chosen: C, a charcoal hero (reopens D46). Rejected: a 4:5 card, a marigold fill, category colours, sensor tilt |
| 09 | [Colour in fintech: colour mass, category colours, a dark hero](case-studies/09-colour-in-fintech.md) | Proposed: colour mass from value, not hue (the dark end of the scale as the brand surface); a dark `inverse` hero in light, pending a blind read (reverses D46); keep the warm neutrals. Category colours: no-go for v1; at most 6 low-chroma tints, never red, green or marigold, if a 5-user search test shows a gain |
| 08 | [Colour retrospective and the grey ramp](case-studies/08-colour-retrospective.md) | Built: `inverse` token for the snackbar (D105). Approved: role-first, measured colour process. Proposed: fill the 0.86→0.46 `L` gap in the grey ramp (separate task) |
| 07 | [Design systems: Groww, Material 3, Primer, Radix, Atlassian, Blade](case-studies/07-design-systems-groww-material-primer-radix.md) | Proposed: set every Material role, one contrast-pair list for all themes, focus as its own token, tinted transparent token. ## Adding a study
1. Copy the structure of `case-studies/01-ask-ai-choice-screen.md`: the job of the screen, what's wrong, research by source, patterns compared, recommendation, risks.
2. Use the next free number. Never renumber.
3. Link every claim to its source. Say when a source couldn't be read (a login wall, or only board pages), rather than guessing what it shows.
4. Add any new, reusable principle to `principles.md` and any new site to `sources.md`.
5. When a recommendation is approved, add a `DECISIONS.md` row, update `UI_DESIGN.md`, and set the study's outcome above.
