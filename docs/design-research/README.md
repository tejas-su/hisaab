# Design research

UX/UI research for Hisaab: case studies of other apps, the principles we drew from them, and where to look. This folder records why a design was chosen. What was decided lives in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, and the resulting spec in `docs/UI_DESIGN.md`.

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| [principles.md](principles.md) | Design principles learned from research, each with its source and the Hisaab screens it applies to |
| [sources.md](sources.md) | Where to look (Apple HIG, Material, Mobbin, Dribbble, Pinterest, NN/g, pattern libraries), what each is good for, and access limits |
| [case-studies/](case-studies/) | One study per design question, numbered `NN-topic.md` |

| 34 | [Fix my number: can people answer "How much money do you have right now?" in one number](case-studies/34-fix-my-number-research.md) | Desk research (YNAB reconcile, BudgetBakers Wallet, Money Lover, Cashew, Goodbudget, PocketGuard, Monekin's issue; Copilot, Monzo and Simplifi for spending from a pot; NPCI's UPI balance-check cap and two US vendor polls). Found: every app asks per account and keeps the gap as a visible adjustment; none asks one number across accounts, so hisaab's question has no precedent; no Indian evidence on whether people know a total; SPEC BR-3 counts only this cycle's *Got*, so a buffer makes the real figure always higher and the *higher* branch could post the whole buffer. Wording to start from: the question plus "Bank and cash together. We'll take out your bills and savings."; one number, not two fields. Five-person test plan written for the owner (privately-checked gap, blind read of the words, preview read-back, a P2′ "Used" read). **Test not run.** Gaps: most help pages unreadable (403 or summaries), no Indian app true-up found, no user data |
| 33 | [Product gap analysis: what still makes life hard, and what to add](case-studies/33-product-gap-analysis.md) | Internal analysis of SPEC, PLAN, DECISIONS and case study 31, plus a check of the code. Proposed, in order: *Fix my number* true-up, opt-in evening nudge, yearly and occasional bills; then payday close and carry-over, *Set a limit*, CSV export, a backup safety net, an app lock. Not approved; handed to `design-product`. Gaps: no user evidence |
| 32 | [Your usual: suggesting the right spend at the right time](case-studies/32-your-usual.md) | Desk research (iOS Siri Suggestions, Android app predictions, Google Maps, Firefox frecency, Spendee, Wallet and Money Manager; Gajos 2008, Findlater 2009/2010 on adaptive UIs; Haggag & Paci on default anchoring; Petrova 2026 and the EFF on creepiness; circular KDE). Tested the unbuilt `docs/features/habits/` design against them. Found: no expense tracker suggests by time of day; adaptive UIs help at 70–79% accuracy and copying beats moving; suggested amounts anchor behaviour; unexplained accuracy feels creepy. **Kept:** ≥ 70% on one amount, top-1 only, a fixed slot, 14-day half-life, half-hour circular buckets, warm-up and dismissal FSM. **Changed:** a pooled fallback when a weekday or weekend profile is thin; *Your usual* with *Forget* in the category sheet; tracked items retired (D138); the pill in every way into Add (D147); a test that the amount was really logged. Phases: pill, then Home question, then bill finder. Mockup 40. **Approved (D153):** BR-17, FR-AD-10, FR-SE-2 AC-6; layout B (keypad 8dp lower); CLAUDE.md's entry-speed exception. Not built. Gaps: no Indian app evidence, no user test of one-tap adds |
| 31 | [Where hisaab stands among popular Play Store expense trackers](case-studies/31-play-store-competitive-landscape.md) | Play listings scripted on 2026-11-11 (Money Manager, Wallet, Monefy, axio 10M+; Money Lover 5M+; Spendee 1M+; Cashew 500K+ at 4.87; Fold; PennyWise) plus desk research, mostly vendor-written roundups. Found five camps: manual ledgers, feature-rich hybrids, SMS capture, Account Aggregator, daily-number apps. No popular India-first Android app owns "how much today?". Gaps: manual-only entry, no widget or quick tile, English only, no logging nudge, no app lock, no social proof. Proposed: next a widget, a QS tile, Hindi and an opt-in evening nudge; explore assisted capture from payment notifications (not SMS), voice and a device-credential lock; later CSV import and export; never bank sync, accounts, cloud, in-app AI or ads. Owner answers: never monetised, Hindi and notification capture later. **Built into the spec:** the widget, tile and app shortcut (D147, mockup 38). Gaps: no installs, screens or interviews |
| 30 | [Play Store colours: the real light-theme colours of 16 shipped apps](case-studies/30-play-store-colours.md) | Measured by script from Google Play screenshots (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED, Groww, Kite, Jupiter, slice, Wise, Monzo, Monefy, Money Manager, Splitwise, Spendee, Claude, Notion; Revolut all dark, Navi too small, Fi and Cashew not on Play). Median 5x5 patches, WCAG and OKLCH computed; text colours and separation are weak. Found: 8 of 16 use white page on white cards, 7 use a grey or tinted page with white cards at a step of only 1.05 to 1.11, and only CRED, Jupiter and Claude are warm (R-B 5 to 9, chroma 0.005 to 0.008). **hisaab's page is the darkest (L 0.943 vs 0.960+), its step the strongest (1.17 vs 1.11 max) and its warmth the highest (R-B 13) of any sampled app**; candidate B sits inside the warm cluster, A and C outside it. Low-contrast accents ship as fills with a label, never as text (Wise lime 1.47:1 is the marigold precedent); CRED's action colour is near-black, which would conflict with the one-signal rule. Ideas: lighten the page and lower the step, keep cards neutral white, stay in the warm cluster. Nothing adopted. Gaps: marketing mockups, one screen per app, no device |
| 29 | [Colour across screens: LCD, AMOLED, Vivid modes, Night Light, daylight](case-studies/29-colour-across-screens.md) | Desk research plus my own arithmetic (Android colour-management docs, Flutter issue 153517, Apple HIG, WCAG and APCA, DisplayMate, Digit's moto g45 review, Material dark theme, user reports on OLED near-black). Found: Flutter draws sRGB on Android, and a non-colour-managed or Vivid display stretches it (marigold turns slightly more orange, luminance about +1%, so the ink-edge rule holds); glare adds light, so every text pair falls below 4.5:1 outdoors and nothing in colour can fix it, but the light layers keep at least 7% luminance step at flare 1.0 while the dark layers drop to about 1%; `#131210` has the same luminance as Material's `#121212`; the warm tint (chroma 0.011) is smaller than a plausible white-point spread, so it is flavour and never meaning. The moto g45 is an IPS LCD (about 220 nits indoors, 482 in sun), so no AMOLED check exists. Per-panel tuning is impossible on Android. A, B and C share luminances, so only tint separates them; C holds up best on cool panels, A in the middle, B worst. Adds principle 85 and a 10-step device protocol; nothing adopted. Gaps: no device or meter, no white-point data, no India AMOLED share, no Xiaomi/Realme mode gamuts |
| 28 | [Light-theme colours: warm neutrals in other systems, three candidate palettes](case-studies/28-light-theme-colours.md) | Desk research (Figma blog and forum, Tailwind v4 OKLCH, Radix sand, Open Props, Polaris, Fluent 2, Spectrum, Atlassian, Ant, GitLab, Twilio, Material HCT, Linear, Stripe, Lyft, Evil Martians, APCA, Anthropic's palette). Figma's own writing gives structure, not neutral values. Found: hisaab's layer step (1.17), ink, ink2 and hairline sit inside the range of peers; its **page tint is the outlier** (about 1.6 to 3.7 times Tailwind's warm-neutral chroma at the same lightness, gap 13 against 2 to 5 in Radix, GitLab, Notion, Anthropic); peers grade the tint (nearly neutral at the page, strongest mid-ramp); marigold tops out near 1.83:1 on white, so no palette lets it sit bare. Proposed for measuring: A keep D108 and alias `displayInk2` to `ink2`, B a graded quieter paper (touches D108's 75° hue), C a stronger cream (the tint D108 rejected). Nothing adopted; contrast figures are hand-calculated and need the `better-colors` skill. Gaps: no Figma neutral values, no sourced values for Things, Bear, Wise, Revolut, CRED, Fi, Jupiter, Kite, no device test |

## Adding a study
1. Copy the structure of `case-studies/01-ask-ai-choice-screen.md`: the job of the screen, what's wrong, research by source, patterns compared, recommendation, risks.
2. Use the next free number. Never renumber.
3. Link every claim to its source. Say when a source couldn't be read (a login wall, or only board pages), rather than guessing what it shows.
4. Add any new, reusable principle to `principles.md` and any new site to `sources.md`.
5. When a recommendation is approved, add a `DECISIONS.md` row, update `UI_DESIGN.md`, and set the study's outcome above.
